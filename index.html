Bún Chả

Bún chả is a dish from Hanoi. It comes with grilled pork in a bowl of sweet and sour dipping sauce, with rice noodles and fresh herbs on the side.

What I love about bún chả is making each bite a little different. I can dip a few noodles into the sauce, add a piece of smoky pork, or tuck in a mint leaf for a fresh taste.
