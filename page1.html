Com Tam (Broken Rice)

Com Tam is one of my favorite Vietnamese dishes. It is especially popular in Southern Vietnam, where I grew up.

What is Com Tam?

Com Tam is made with broken rice and is usually served with grilled pork, a fried egg, vegetables, and fish sauce.

Why I Love It

I love Com Tam because it is simple but full of flavor. The grilled pork and fish sauce taste great together. It also reminds me of home and the food I grew up eating in Vietnam.
