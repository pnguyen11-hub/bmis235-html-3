Pho (Vietnamese Noodle Soup)

Pho is one of the most famous dishes in Vietnam. It is also one of my favorite foods, especially when I want something warm and comforting.

What is Pho?

Pho is a Vietnamese noodle soup made with rice noodles, flavorful broth, and beef or chicken. It is usually served with fresh herbs, lime, and other vegetables.

Why I Love It

I love Pho because of its rich broth and fresh ingredients. Growing up in Vietnam, eating a hot bowl of Pho was always something I enjoyed. Now that I am studying in the United States, Pho reminds me of home and my family.
