Bun Cha

Grilled pork and rice noodles from Hanoi, Vietnam.

What comes with it?

The grilled pork comes in a small bowl of sweet and sour dipping sauce. The rice noodles and fresh herbs come on a separate plate. To eat it, dip a few noodles into the bowl and pick up a piece of pork.

Why I love it

My favorite part is the smoky edge of the grilled pork. I like to add a mint leaf to my next bite so the noodles taste a little different each time.
