Bun Cha (Grilled Pork with Noodles)

Bun Cha is a traditional Vietnamese dish from Hanoi, the capital of Vietnam. It is one of my favorite Vietnamese foods because of its unique flavors.

What is Bun Cha?

Bun Cha is made with grilled pork, rice noodles, fresh herbs, and a bowl of sweet and savory fish sauce. The pork is grilled until it is slightly crispy and full of flavor.

Why I Love It

I love Bun Cha because the combination of grilled pork, fresh noodles, and fish sauce tastes amazing. Even though I grew up in Southern Vietnam, I have always enjoyed this dish from Northern Vietnam. It reminds me of the different food traditions across my home country.
